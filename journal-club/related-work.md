## Detail on the two most relevant

- **PMID 42703471** - Punyo, Yadav, Krishna, Maurya, Jain, Jain. *Surgical Management of Pilonidal Sinus: A Comparative Study of Excision, Limberg Flap, and Keystone Flap Reconstruction.* **Cureus, 2026.** Prospective comparative observational study, 46 patients, tertiary care centre, February 2024 to July 2025. Recurrence 13.3% occurred only in the simple excision group (p < 0.001); flap procedures had superior scar outcomes (p = 0.014) and satisfaction (p = 0.002) but longer operative time and hospital stay (p < 0.001) and more complications (43.5% overall, seroma 28.3%).
- **PMID 42700095** - Osmanov, Hazir, Ekici. *Comparative outcomes of phenol therapy and Karydakis flap in pilonidal sinus disease: A retrospective cohort study with 1-year follow-up.* **Medicine, 2026.** Retrospective cohort, 125 consecutive patients (77 Karydakis flap, 48 phenol), January 2024 to January 2025, at least 1 year of follow-up. Wound healing significantly faster with phenol (15.4 +/- 8.4 vs 36.1 +/- 7.6 days, P < .005); 1-year recurrence 12.5% (phenol) vs 9.1% (Karydakis), not significantly different (P = .09).

**Note on relevance:** the search also returned PMID 42732549 on hidradenitis suppurativa, which is related only in that pilonidal sinus appears as an associated condition; it is not a surgical treatment outcome study and should not be treated as directly relevant evidence.
